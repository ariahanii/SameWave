# Overall Structure
The number of open-ended questions to ask is 3
The questions are the following
* "You wake up and gravity is sideways for exactly one hour. Walk me through what you do."
* "Pick a household object to be your spirit animal. Defend your choice."
* "Imagine aliens attacked planet earth and they are all in shape of geese(plural of goose) and goats how would you approach to fight against them?"

The Axis are
|-----|-------|---------|
|Axis	|1 (low)	|10 (high)|
|practicality	|Whimsical, ignores real-world logistics	|Grounded, treats it like a real problem|
|Focus	|Self-focused (what I'd do)	|World-focused (how it affects everyone)|
|elaboration	|One-liner, minimal detail	|Builds a full scenario with steps/narrative|
|assertiveness	|Avoidant, diplomatic, sidesteps conflict	|Decisive, confrontational, bold|
